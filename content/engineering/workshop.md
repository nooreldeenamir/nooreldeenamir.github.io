# AI and software: an internal workshop for Sahahly interns

By Nooreldeen Abdelkarim · Record published 11 November 2026

I led an internal AI and software session for Sahahly's interns, introducing machine learning, generative AI, large language models and prompt engineering. We explored how those ideas connect to software products and how AI is applied at Sahahly. The session included interactive activities, questions and a live demonstration.

My role was to explain the concepts, lead the session and connect the discussion to a practical product context.

## Topics

- The relationship between AI, machine learning and generative AI.
- What LLMs contribute to an application, and where conventional software remains necessary.
- How prompt wording and context influence an interaction.
- The need to inspect model output and keep human judgement involved.
- Moving from an AI demonstration to a usable software product.

## Teaching approach

The session combined explanations with audience participation and a live demo. Those formats let the discussion move between terminology and concrete examples, rather than relying on definitions alone.

For a future session, I would first ask about participants' programming and AI experience, then adapt the technical depth. I would also make a small hands-on exercise available so participants can change an input, inspect the result and discuss what still needs checking. These are proposed improvements, not claims about activities already completed.
